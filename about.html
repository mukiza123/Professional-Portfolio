Impact-driven work

My work is guided by a simple principle: understand how software behaves in real environments, and how it can solve practical problems. That drives every project, whether I am configuring networked services, debugging system issues, or designing offline-capable tools.

Beyond the technical side, I want to build systems that are inclusive, reliable and human-centred. Software engineering is not only about writing code. It is about building things that last, serve, and hold up in use.

Mission

To empower African youth in rural public schools with technology that unlocks their potential, turns their talents into tangible opportunities, and equips them to become changemakers who uplift their communities and shape the future of Africa.
